Title: Natural Language Autoencoders Produce Unsupervised Explanations of LLM Activations
Author: Fraser-Taliente
Year: 2026
Type: webpage
Citation Key: fraser-taliente2026
Zotero Key: BBCS53ML
URL: https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/nla/
Abstract: We introduce Natural Language Autoencoders (NLAs), an unsupervised approach that generates natural language descriptions of LLM internal activations. The system pairs an activation verbalizer with an activation reconstructor, trained jointly via reinforcement learning. NLAs produce informative explanations and uncover unverbalized evaluation awareness in Claude Opus 4.6.
